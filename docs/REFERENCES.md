# Sources and reading map

These links were checked for discovery/documentation on October 14, 2026.
Abstracts support broad comparison values and scope; they are not a substitute
for reading full methods before reproducing a specific algorithm/action.

- Hasenbusch, Pinn, Vinti (1999), [Critical exponents of the three-dimensional
  Ising universality class](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9806012),
  [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.59.11471).
  Proposal's principal reference; inspect action conventions and corrections.
- Hasenbusch (2010), [Finite-size scaling study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.4486).
  Reference nu/eta/omega and correction analysis; different microscopic models
  do not supply a Tc for our couplings.
- Metropolis et al. (1953), Equation of state calculations by fast computing
  machines, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1699114).
  Proposal's sampler reference; bibliographic pointer, full text not reviewed here.
- Wolff (2004), [Monte Carlo errors with less errors](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0306017).
  Autocorrelation/error analysis; read full methods for advanced estimators.
- [Missouri S&T Mill quick start](https://itrss.mst.edu/cluster/quickstart/).
  SSH, transfers, sbatch, and queue inspection; verify live site settings.
- [Python venv](https://docs.python.org/3/library/venv.html).
  Local environment isolation.
- [CMake presets](https://cmake.org/cmake/help/v3.22/manual/cmake-presets.7.html).
  Shared presets versus private machine settings.
